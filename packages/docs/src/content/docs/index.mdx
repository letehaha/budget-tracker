New to MoneyMatter? The [quick start](/getting-started/quick-start/) walks you from sign-up to your first transaction in a few minutes. If a number in your reports ever surprises you, [key concepts](/getting-started/key-concepts/) explains how accounts, transfers, refunds and stats fit together.

You can open this help center from the app at any time: click your email address (or your initial, if the sidebar is collapsed) at the bottom of the sidebar, then choose **Help & docs**.
